The Art of Sozni Embroidery

Originating from the heart of the Kashmir valley, our watches feature the rare 'Sozni' embroidery technique. Each piece is meticulously hand-stitched by master artisans who have preserved this needlework heritage for generations.

Unlike machine-made patterns, our silk thread work creates a 3D tactile texture that catches the light beautifully, making each watch a unique masterpiece of wearable art. The vibrant motifs are inspired by the flora of the Mughal gardens, reflecting a poise that is both maternal and vibrantly artistic.
