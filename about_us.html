OUR HERITAGE

Our Story: Empowering Women, One Thread at a Time

Every stitch we make is a testament to the resilience and artistry of the women of Gujarat, blending centuries-old tradition with modern elegance.

Preserving Traditions, Creating Independence

At KEVILAGU, our mission transcends mere aesthetics. We are a collective dedicated to the financial independence of Gujarati women. By providing a global platform for their ancestral craft, we ensure that the rhythmic art of embroidery continues to thrive in a digital age.

Each piece is handcrafted in rural workshops where our artisans are paid fair wages, provided with safe working environments, and encouraged to lead their communities. Your purchase directly fuels education initiatives and healthcare for their families.
