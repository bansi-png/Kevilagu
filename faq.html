Frequently Asked Questions

Shipping Information

expand_more
We offer free shipping across India on all orders. Each KEVILAGU piece is handmade to order, ensuring the highest quality. Please allow 7-10 business days for artisanal crafting and delivery.

Returns & Exchanges

expand_more
Due to the artisanal nature of our products, we offer returns only for damaged items within 48 hours of delivery. Please provide a clear unboxing video for a smooth exchange process.

COD & Payments

expand_more
We offer Cash on Delivery (COD) for most pin codes across India. You can also pay securely using UPI, Credit Cards, or Net Banking for a faster checkout experience.

Sizing Guide

expand_more
Our pieces follow a relaxed, contemporary fit. Please refer to our detailed sizing chart on the product page. For custom tailoring, please contact our support team.

We're here to help

Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 6 PM IST. We speak English, Hindi, and Gujarati.
